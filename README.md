## Featured Project: Big Mountain Pricing Analysis

### Project Objective

The goal of this project was to determine whether Big Mountain Resort is appropriately priced relative to its facilities and to evaluate how operational changes could impact ticket pricing and revenue.

### Key Findings

- Big Mountain appeared to be underpriced relative to comparable resorts
- The model suggested a supported ticket price closer to $96 compared to the current $81 ticket price
- Infrastructure improvements such as adding runs and chair lifts could increase supported pricing and revenue
- Large run closures negatively impacted both ticket pricing and projected revenue

### Business Recommendations

- Gradually increase ticket pricing while monitoring customer response
- Carefully evaluate infrastructure investment costs before expansion
- Avoid major run closures due to their negative revenue impact

### Future Improvements

Potential future enhancements include:
- Incorporating weather and customer behavior data
- Adding competitor pricing analysis
- Deploying the model as an interactive dashboard or web application
